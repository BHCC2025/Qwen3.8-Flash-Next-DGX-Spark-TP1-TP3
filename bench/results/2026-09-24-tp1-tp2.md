Notes:
- TP1 keeps the 47.7 GiB n-gram table on NVMe and reads rows on demand (`PLE_MODE=staged`), so it is the slowest.
  It is also the only way to fit the model on one Spark.
- On prefill, TP2 is slightly faster than TP3 (2,882 vs 2,669 tok/s at 8K): one fabric link and no padding
  overhead. TP3 decodes faster and is the only one with 1M context.
- The upstream single-Spark recipe reports 43.9 tok/s (TP1) and 53.7 tok/s (TP2) as medians of its own 40-prompt
  harness. Those are a different prompt mix, not a contradiction of the numbers above.
